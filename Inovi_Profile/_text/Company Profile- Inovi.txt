Company Profile
1. Company Overview

Inovi Solutions is a Texas-based minority-owned and women-owned business
specializing in enterprise Salesforce solutions, artificial intelligence (AI), cloud
ERP, data engineering, and enterprise systems integration. Founded in 2011
(subject to confirmation), Inovi helps organizations modernize business
processes, connect disparate systems, automate workflows, and improve
operational efficiency.

Inovi combines expertise in Salesforce implementation and development
with AI-enabled applications, ERP solutions, data management, and
enterprise integration. Its multidisciplinary team supports projects from
requirements analysis and solution design through implementation, testing,
deployment, and ongoing support.
