1. Purpose & Focus:
This matrix is the primary tool the Government will use to conduct market research and refine the acquisition strategy for the MCCE OSS procurement.
Tech-Enabled Labor Focus: As noted in RFI Section 1.1, the Government is seeking a tech-enabled labor force, not a standalone software purchase. Your responses must describe how your professional labor force will deliver services and utilize automation/AI tools (either your proprietary corporate tools at no additional cost to the Government, or existing Government-owned platforms) to drive down labor costs over time.
2. Formatting and Submission Constraints (Strictly Enforced):
300-Word Response Limit: Your response in each "Vendor Response" cell must not exceed 300 words. Any text exceeding this limit may not be read or evaluated by the Government. Keep responses concise, objective, and outcome-oriented.
No Structural Alterations: Vendors are strictly prohibited from modifying the structure of this spreadsheet. Do NOT add rows, insert new columns, merge cells, change font sizes, or embed objects, hyperlinked files, or images. Responses must be typed as plain text directly into the designated "Vendor Response" column.
Primary Evaluation Weight: The Government’s primary review will focus exclusively on this matrix. Supplementary materials (e.g., white papers, slide decks) submitted alongside this matrix are strictly limited to a 15-page maximum and will be reviewed solely at the Government’s discretion.
3. Submission Protocols:
Due Date: Your completed matrix must be submitted electronically via email to the Contracting Officer no later than 30 calendar days post-release of the RFI.
