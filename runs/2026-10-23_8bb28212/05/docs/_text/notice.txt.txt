 Dear Industry Partners, 

 The Department of Homeland Security (DHS), Office of Procurement Operations (OPO), on behalf of the DHS Office of the Chief Information Officer, intends to issue a solicitation to obtain Network, Cloud, and Cybersecurity Services (NCCS) 2.0.  Attached is a final draft solicitation for the requirement for your review. The resultant contract will be a DHS Department Wide Single Award Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract. 

 The notional timeline of key events is as follows: 

 
	  Questions due on final draft solicitation:  9/22/2026 
	  Final solicitation release:  10/1/2026 
	  Proposal due date Phase 1:  10/6/2026 
	  Government notification to proceed to Phase 2:  10/9/2026 
	  Proposal due date Phase 2:  10/21/2026 
	  Anticipated Award:  Late November 2026 
 

  

 Questions and comments are due no later than 12:00 p.m. Eastern Time Tuesday September 22, 2026. Questions and comments shall be submitted electronically via electronic mail to Nicole Belanger,  Nicole.Belanger@hq.dhs.gov  and Michael Lipperini,  Michael.Lipperini@hq.dhs.gov.  Electronic mail attachments, if included, shall be prepared using Microsoft Office. 

 Please continue to check this notice for further updates on this procurement.
