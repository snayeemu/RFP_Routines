Advance Registration and Appointment Confirmation is required: 

The following rules apply to the Network Operations Security Center (NOSC) Network, Cloud, and Cybersecurity Services (NCCS) Reading Room:

Access to the Reading Room is strictly limited to vendors who have submitted registration requests not less than 24-hours in advance of their desired appointment time. 

Appointments will be made in 30-minute increments.

Reservations will be confirmed on a first-come, first-serve basis.  To make reservations, please email TBD

Vendors must have received written appointment confirmations via email from the Office of the Chief Information Officer (OCIO) prior to their requested reservation time.

The number of vendor representatives permitted access to the Reading Room is limited to two (2) individuals, as named in the reservation request.  Substitutions will be allowed not less than 48-hours in advance of the confirmed reservation time/date.

At the designated day/time of the reservation, Vendor representatives listed in the reservation shall be admitted into the Reading Room to review the material made available. 

Vendors must be prepared to show a Government-issued identification such as a Driver’s License and a company-issued identification such as a badge or presentation card.

Procedures: 

Vendors with confirmed appointments shall arrive at the designated Lorton facility.  Upon arrival, vendor representatives will ring the doorbell, sign in to the logbook, and wait for an OCIO representative escort. A DHS OCIO representative will escort vendors to the Reading Room.  Vendors shall be required to sign in with their name and company.

A DHS OCIO monitor will remain with vendors while inside of the Reading Room to oversee events. The monitor/escort will not be available to answer any questions or address any comments related to the documents provided in the Reading Room. Vendors are requested to submit any questions related to the documents in accordance with the solicitation instructions. 

All reading room documents will be presented in hard copy format provided by DHS OCIO. 

The following personal vendor items will not be allowed in the Reading Room: 

Cell phones
Tablets
Laptops
USB flash drives
 
Vendors may bring notebooks or other writing materials to make personal notes.
