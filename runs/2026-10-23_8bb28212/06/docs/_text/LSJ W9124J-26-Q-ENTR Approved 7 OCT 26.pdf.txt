   5.3, 5.6): Tiered technical troubleshooting, hardware/software maintenance, ticket

1. Substantial Transformation (>25% Rule): Under 13 CFR § 124.504(d)(1),
   where a procurement consolidates work previously performed under separate
   contracts and introduces newly mandated, enterprise-wide technical
   architectures (AUDS, MAPS under DoD IL5, Vasion Print, and CSfC/WINDAR-S)
   that exceed 25% of the baseline scope, operational reach, and technical
   complexity of the predecessor efforts, the procurement is legally recognized as a
   "new requirement." This overarching determination applies across all
   predecessor work, including standalone contracts W9124J-24-C-0007 and
   W9124J-24-C-0004.

2. Task Order Carve-Out for W9124J-23-F-0047 (13 CFR § 124.504(d)(4)(i)): It is
   specifically noted that predecessor contract W9124J-23-F-0047 was issued as
   an individual task order under the GSA STARS III Government-Wide Acquisition
   Contract (GWAC) 47QTCB21D0359 (whereas 24-C-0007 and 24-C-0004 were
   standalone contracts). Pursuant to 13 CFR § 124.504(d)(4)(i):

   "The requirement that a follow-on procurement must be released from the 8(a)
   program in order for it to be fulfilled outside the 8(a) program does not apply to
   task or delivery orders offered to and accepted into the 8(a) program, where the
   basic contract was not accepted into the 8(a) program." Consequently, task order
   W9124J-23-F-0047 possesses an independent, secondary regulatory exemption
   from 8(a) release mandates.

3. No Requirement to Await SBA Concurrence (Court of Federal Claims
   Precedent): Under controlling federal court precedent (Vinsys Information
   Technology v. United States, 151 Fed. Cl. 678), when a Contracting Officer
   reasonably determines that an acquisition is a "new requirement," the agency is
   not required to request, wait for, or obtain formal release or affirmative
   concurrence from the SBA. In accordance with 13 CFR § 124.504(d)(1), the
   agency’s sole procedural obligation is to provide formal written notification to the
   servicing SBA District Office and the SBA Procurement Center Representative
   (PCR) prior to award, documenting that the procurement represents a new
   requirement being fulfilled outside the 8(a) program. Transmittal of this written
   notice completes the agency's coordination requirement, permitting the
   Contracting Officer to proceed immediately with contract award.
